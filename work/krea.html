Krea

Personalization & image generation

Concepted and proposed the moodboarding and personalization feature for Krea’s K2 in-house image generation model. Collaborated with the Krea team to design and implement the experience.

Developed the approach for users to import visual references from platforms such as Cosmos and Pinterest, and designed an embedding and clustering system to identify themes across those references. Combined these signals with LLM-generated representations of a user’s taste and style, which feed as inputs to K2 to generate imagery aligned with the user’s visual preferences.
