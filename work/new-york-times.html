The New York Times

Applied LLMs & editorial tools

Worked with The New York Times’ licensing and editorial teams on applied LLM workflows designed to augment existing editorial processes. Developed and tested a human-in-the-loop system that helped editors identify potential cuts to meet word-count requirements, based on a workflow requested by the editorial team.

Also contributed to the engineering and evaluation of an LLM-powered metric conversion pipeline for NYT Cooking, enabling automated conversion of recipe measurements for international audiences.
