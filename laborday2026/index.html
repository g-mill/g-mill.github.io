TRANSPORTATION

We have scheduled a charter bus from Manhattan to Camp Starlight. The bus will leave from Manhattan Friday mid-afternoon and will leave Camp Starlight on Monday morning. The round-trip cost for the charter bus will be approximately $80.00 per person.

LINENS

Linens will be available to rent for $15.00. The rental will include a pillow, blanket, sheet and towel.

MEALS & GRATUITY

We will be requesting approximately $50.00 from our guests to cover all meals and gratuity for the amazing Camp Starlight staff who will be working to make this event happen.
