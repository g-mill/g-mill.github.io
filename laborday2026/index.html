TRANSPORTATION

We have scheduled a charter bus from Manhattan to Camp Starlight. The bus will leave from Manhattan Friday mid-afternoon and will leave Camp Starlight on Monday morning. The round-trip cost for the charter bus will be approximately $80.00 per person.

ROOM & BOARD

Accommodations, meals, alcohol, linens, and gratuity for the Camp Starlight staff helping make the weekend possible will be included in the total cost for each guest.

PLUS ONES
