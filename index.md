# Griffin Miller

AI engineer and musician in New York. This is the Markdown version of <https://griffinmiller.com/> for AI agents and other text readers, with a short Q&A at the end.

I'm an AI engineer based in New York. I work on AI/ML and product at [Cosmos](https://griffinmiller.com/work/cosmos). I also work with companies on applied AI strategy, product development, ML architecture, and custom tooling.

I studied computer science and classical music at Stanford. I'm a classical orchestral timpanist, percussionist, and conductor. My favorite instrument to play is [marimba](https://griffinmiller.com/music/marimba).

I'm constantly figuring out how to incorporate music into my life. I recently picked up the flute and guitar and lugged a [butter yellow spinet piano](https://griffinmiller.com/music/piano) into my apartment. I've also been messing around with music production across genres. I also have an [electronic marimba](https://griffinmiller.com/music/malletstation) here in the city that I'd love to use more.

I'm also an adjunct professor at Fordham University and Hunter College, where I teach computer science.

## Research & projects at Stanford

### Sidekick

A replication study of "Sidekick: In-Network Assistance for Secure End-to-End Transport Protocols" (NSDI '24).

[Repo](https://github.com/cs244-team/sidekick) · [Paper](https://reproducingnetworkresearch.wordpress.com/2024/06/08/cs-244-24-replicating-results-from-sidekick-in-network-assistance-for-secure-end-to-end-transport-protocols/)

### I2S Speaker & Amplifier

Functional Adafruit MAX98357 I2S Class-D Mono Amplifier and Knowles SPH0645LM4H-B Microphone. Runs on top of a simple, clean OS for the widely used, ARM-based Raspberry Pi that I built from scratch over the course of a quarter.

[Repo](https://github.com/g-mill/speaker)

### SecurityTok

Evaluating Pro-Security Advice on TikTok. A qualitative study of the quality of modern technological security education on social media.

[Paper (PDF)](https://griffinmiller.com/media/SecurityTok.pdf)
